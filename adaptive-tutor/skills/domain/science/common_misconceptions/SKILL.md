# Common Science Misconceptions

## How to Use This File
When you detect a question or statement that suggests one of these misconceptions,
address it directly and gently BEFORE answering the student's actual question.
Frame it as "a precision point" not a correction.

## Biology Misconceptions

| Misconception | What to Say Instead |
|---|---|
| "Osmosis is when things move through a membrane" | "Osmosis is specifically about water moving to where there's more dissolved stuff — other molecules move by diffusion or active transport" |
| "We only use 10% of our brain" | "We use all regions of our brain — different parts activate for different tasks" |
| "Evolution means an organism adapts during its lifetime" | "Evolution happens across generations, not within one organism's lifetime" |
| "Antibiotics kill viruses" | "Antibiotics only work on bacteria — viruses need antiviral medications or your immune system" |
| "Plants get their food from the soil" | "Plants make their own food from CO2 and light — soil provides minerals, not food" |

## Physics Misconceptions

| Misconception | What to Say Instead |
|---|---|
| "Objects in motion need a force to keep moving" | "An object keeps moving unless something stops it — friction is that something on Earth" |
| "Electricity is used up in a circuit" | "Electrical energy is converted to other forms — the electrons keep cycling" |
| "Sound travels faster than light" | "Light travels about 900,000 times faster than sound" |
| "Cold is a thing that enters warm objects" | "Cold is the absence of heat — heat moves from warm to cold, not the other way" |

## Chemistry Misconceptions

| Misconception | What to Say Instead |
|---|---|
| "Mixing bleach and ammonia is just slightly dangerous" | "This creates toxic chloramine gas — it is seriously harmful" |
| "Chemical reactions always produce heat" | "Endothermic reactions absorb heat and feel cold to the touch" |
