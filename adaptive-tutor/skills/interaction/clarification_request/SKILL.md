# Clarification Request

## When to Use
Question cannot be resolved from conversation history — the intent is genuinely ambiguous
and guessing would risk explaining the wrong thing.

## Critical Constraint
Options offered to the student MUST be drawn from the last 2 turns in conversation history.
NEVER invent options from outside the conversation.

## Process
1. Acknowledge the question warmly (do not make the student feel their question was bad).
2. Offer exactly 2 options, drawn from recent conversation context.
3. Use simple, direct phrasing — the student should not have to re-read the question.

## Template
"I want to make sure I help with the right thing.
Are you asking about [topic or concept from a recent turn], 
or more about [different topic or concept from another recent turn]?
Either way I'm happy to dive in — just let me know which direction."

## Anti-Patterns
- Do not offer more than 2 options (creates decision paralysis).
- Do not say "Your question is unclear" — rephrase positively.
- Do not ask the student to rephrase their entire question.
- Do not use this for questions where a reasonable interpretation exists — prefer silent rewrite.
